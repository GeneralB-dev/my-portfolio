How it works

The tool compares incoming demand (customers per hour) against your team's total service capacity (staff × how many people each can serve per hour). When demand exceeds capacity, a queue builds — the calculator estimates how long that wait grows and flags whether you're understaffed, balanced, or over-resourced.

It's a simplified version of the queuing logic used in real operations planning — the same kind of thinking behind the restaurant and hospital process work in the other case studies.
