About

Background

My path has run through product design, operations, and administration. I've led teams, conducted user research, analyzed data to guide decisions, and streamlined processes across very different environments — from hospital and restaurant operations to compliance and administrative work with the Nigeria Customs Service.

In product design, I lean on human-centered design principles — using research and data to guide decisions, then tools like Figma and Adobe Illustrator to move from wireframes to polished, intuitive experiences. I also bring visual storytelling into the mix through video editing, which sharpens how I think about pacing and communicating ideas clearly.

I do my best work where design, operations, and customer experience meet.
