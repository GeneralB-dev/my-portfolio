For my church community · Filmed and edited independently

Children's Day

A community video for Children's Day at my church — filmed and edited to capture the day's energy for the congregation to relive and share.

Approach

Both projects follow the same process: shoot with the edit already in mind — covering wide shots for context and close, candid moments for emotion — then build the story in CapCut around pacing and music rather than a strict shot-by-shot log. For the MRC piece, the goal was momentum: short cuts that carry the energy of a live summit. For Children's Day, the goal was warmth: longer holds on candid, unscripted moments.
