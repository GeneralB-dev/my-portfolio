Why I built this

At The Rock Hospital, one of the most common friction points was patients not understanding what their HMO would actually cover before a procedure — leading to billing disputes after the fact. This tool models that same coverage-vs-cap logic I worked with directly: a plan covers a percentage of the bill, up to a fixed cap, and the patient covers the rest.

The cap matters as much as the percentage — a plan with 95% coverage but a low cap can leave a patient with a bigger bill than a plan with 80% coverage and a higher cap, which is exactly the kind of thing this tool makes visible instantly instead of requiring a manual calculation.
